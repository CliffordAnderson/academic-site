The [Center of Theological Inquiry](https://www.ctinquiry.org/) in Princeton announced that its Director, William Storrar, had appointed Clifford Anderson as Director of Digital Research. Anderson came to CTI from Vanderbilt University, where he had served as Associate University Librarian for Research and Digital Strategy, and where he would continue as Chief Digital Strategist at the Vanderbilt University Library.

At CTI, Anderson would build a new digital infrastructure for the Center's research program and work with Associate Director Joshua Mauldin on the Inquiry on Thriving in Diverse Contexts, which would launch CTI's new research cycle with both virtual and resident inquiry. Anderson said he looked forward to "assisting with the digital transformation of CTI's research."
